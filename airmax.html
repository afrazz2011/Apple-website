AirPods Max

Premium Build Materials: Apple has used high-quality materials, including stainless steel and aluminum, giving the AirPods Max a premium and durable build.

Adaptive EQ: The headphones use adaptive equalization to adjust the sound in real-time based on the fit and seal of the ear cushions, providing an optimized audio experience.

Seamless Integration with Apple Ecosystem: If you are already using other Apple devices, the AirPods Max seamlessly integrate with them, offering features like automatic device switching and quick pairing.

Intuitive Controls: The AirPods Max come with a Digital Crown for easy control of volume, playback, and Siri activation, offering a user-friendly interface.
