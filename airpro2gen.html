AirPods Pro Generation 2

Active Noise Cancellation (ANC): The AirPods Pro are equipped with ANC, which helps block out external noise for a more immersive audio experience.

Transparency Mode: This feature allows you to hear your surroundings while still listening to music or taking calls, enhancing safety and awareness.

Customizable Fit: The AirPods Pro come with interchangeable ear tips to ensure a comfortable and secure fit for different ear shapes.

Spatial Audio: This feature provides a more immersive audio experience by replicating sound in a three-dimensional space, enhancing the perception of sound coming from different directions.
