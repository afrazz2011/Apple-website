AirPods 2nd Generation

Automatic Ear Detection: The AirPods 2 have sensors that detect when they are in your ears, pausing playback when you take them out and resuming when you put them back in.

Siri Integration: AirPods 2 come with built-in Siri support, allowing you to activate Siri hands-free by saying "Hey Siri." This makes it easy to control music, make calls, and more using voice commands.

Quick and Easy Pairing: Thanks to the Apple-designed H1 chip, AirPods 2 quickly pair with Apple devices when taken out of the charging case. The connection is seamless and designed to work well within the Apple ecosystem.

Wireless Charging Case (Optional): If you opt for the wireless charging case, you can conveniently charge your AirPods by placing the case on a Qi-compatible wireless charger.
